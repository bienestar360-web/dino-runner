📖 About the Dinosaur Game

Play the Dinosaur Game Online

The Dinosaur Game is a free endless runner you can play directly in your browser. Control a T-Rex that runs automatically through a desert landscape; press Spacebar, tap the screen, or use the Up Arrow to jump over cacti and avoid obstacles. The longer you survive, the higher your score climbs on the global leaderboard.

How to Play

Press Spacebar or tap the screen to jump. On desktop, the Up Arrow also works. Avoid obstacles as they appear — cacti on the ground and flying pterosaurs above. Each jump must be timed; mistimed jumps are the most common cause of game over. Collect meat for bonus points, and grab power-ups like the star (invincibility) and magnet (auto-collect nearby items).

Game Modes

MyDinoRun offers seven game modes: Classic, Survival, Carnivore, Fast, Slow, Meteor, and Legend. Classic is the standard endless runner. Survival removes collectibles for a pure dodging challenge. Carnivore turns you into a predator. Meteor accelerates the approaching comet. Legend is for experienced players seeking a real challenge.

Global Leaderboard

Every run is scored against a worldwide leaderboard. Choose a unique alias or let the game assign one automatically. The top three players are displayed after each game, with a crown marking the highest scorer. No account or registration is required to compete.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Dinosaur Game?

The Dinosaur Game is an endless runner featuring a T-Rex that runs automatically through a desert. You jump over cacti and dodge obstacles to survive as long as possible. MyDinoRun is an independent version inspired by the classic Chrome dinosaur game.

Can I play the Dinosaur Game online for free?

Yes. MyDinoRun is completely free. No downloads, no account required, no payment. Just open the page and start playing.

How do I jump in the Dinosaur Game?

Press the Spacebar or the Up Arrow on desktop, or tap the screen on mobile. The T-Rex jumps automatically and lands back on the ground.

Is this the same as the Chrome Dinosaur Game?

It is inspired by the Chrome dinosaur game but not affiliated with Google. MyDinoRun adds extra features like power-ups, multiple game modes, and a global leaderboard.

Does the Dinosaur Game work on mobile?

Yes. The game is fully playable on mobile browsers. Tap the screen to jump. The layout adapts to phones and tablets.
