📖 About the Dinosaur Game

Play the Dinosaur Game Online

MyDinoRun is a free endless runner you can play in your browser. A green dinosaur runs automatically through a volcanic valley while a meteor grows in the sky. Press the Spacebar, or click or tap the screen, to jump over rocks and pterosaurs and survive as long as you can. It is an independent game inspired by the classic Chrome dinosaur game and is not affiliated with Google.

How to Play

Press the Spacebar on desktop, or click or tap the screen, to jump. The jump has a fixed height, so timing is everything. The obstacles are rocks on the ground and pterosaurs flying at a fixed height. You can also land on an enemy to stomp it: it is destroyed, you bounce, and you earn 100 points. Meat gives you 50 points, and you earn an extra life every 10,000 points.

Power-ups

Amber makes you invulnerable for 10 seconds and destroys the enemies you touch (150 points each). The meteor crystal pulls collectibles toward you for 10 seconds. Hearts give you an extra life.

Game Modes

MyDinoRun has seven game modes: Classic, Survival, Carnivore, Fast, Slow, Meteor and Legend. Classic is the standard mode. Survival has no collectibles, so you score only for time and stomps. Carnivore gives no points for time: only meat, stomps and amber kills count. Fast starts at twice the speed of Classic, and Slow starts at a little over half of it. In Meteor mode the background meteor grows twice as fast. Legend starts at double speed, sends enemies twice as often and makes meat worth 100 points.

Global Leaderboard

Every run is saved to a worldwide leaderboard. Choose an alias or let the game assign one, and after each game you see your rank and the global top three. No account or registration is required.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Dinosaur Game?

The Dinosaur Game is a free endless runner you can play in your browser. In MyDinoRun, a green dinosaur runs automatically through a volcanic valley while a meteor grows in the sky, and you jump over rocks and pterosaurs to survive as long as possible.

Can I play the Dinosaur Game online for free?

Yes. MyDinoRun is completely free. There are no downloads, no account and no payment: just open the page and start playing.

How do I jump in the Dinosaur Game?

Press the Spacebar on desktop, or click or tap the screen on mobile. The dinosaur jumps and lands back on the ground, and you can also land on an enemy to stomp it.

Is this the same as the Chrome Dinosaur Game?

No. It is an independent game inspired by the Chrome dinosaur game and is not affiliated with Google. MyDinoRun adds seven game modes, power-ups, a stomp attack and a global leaderboard.

Does the Dinosaur Game work on mobile?

Yes. The game is fully playable on mobile browsers: tap the screen to jump. The layout adapts to phones and tablets.
